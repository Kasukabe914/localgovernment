Data coverage

Population figures are 2024 estimates and land areas are the 2025 values republished in the Department of Internal Affairs council-profile release. Average residential rates are published 2024/25 figures. Net assets are council assets less liabilities at 30 June 2024.

Important limitations

The figures come from different published sources and reference dates. They should not be interpreted as a current financial ranking or a forecast of amalgamation outcomes. 4 councils have no published average residential rates figure in the source used here.
